# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Easy to Figma a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give technical feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy, licensing, and ownership of shared design files.
- Prioritize the health of the project and its community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing proprietary source files without redistribution rights

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. Temporary or permanent bans may be applied when necessary.

Report conduct issues privately to the project maintainer through the contact methods on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
